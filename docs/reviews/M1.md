## Verdict: **changes required**

The auth core is solid. The verifier accepts only asymmetric algorithms, so HS256, `none` and public-key-as-HMAC are all refused. It checks `iss`, `exp` (against the injected clock), `role = authenticated` and a UUID `sub`. Tokens without a `kid` are refused, and the JWKS cache is capped at 10 minutes. Dev auth is refused by `Settings` when `APP_ENV=prod`, and its router is only mounted when `AUTH_MODE=dev`. Both rules are tested. Every 401 is problem+json with `WWW-Authenticate`. Ownership is enforced in the repos: another user's work or profile answers 404, which is tested end to end on Postgres. The candidate search touches no database. Audit rows are transactional and carry no e-mail (only `email_domain`). The SQL is parametrised throughout, including the two `text()` statements. The import-linter contracts are unchanged.

Two things block approval:

1. The W1 claim flow lacks the works-confirmation step that the plan and the screen require.
2. Profile writes (import job, toggle, claim) are not safe under concurrency, so the design invariant "user toggles survive re-import" and the reclaim/at-least-once guarantees can silently break. The job also holds a DB transaction across an OpenAlex call, which the design forbids.
