# M2 B1-B3, B5, B6: vault, ingestion, retention, agent factory (design)

**Goal (plan M2 tasks 1-3, 5, 6).** Private vault: upload PDF/DOCX, ingest as a resumable `vault.ingest` job (extract, chunk with page offsets and stable ids, `InsightExtractor` with map-reduce, verification), apply the retention policy (D6/ADR-009), set the kind, delete with a full cascade. An agent factory on top of `LLMPort`.
**Acceptance.** A 30-page declined proposal PDF reaches `ready` within 2 min, with insights holding aims and verbatim reviewer objections linked to chunk ids. Deleting a document removes the storage object, chunks and insights (integration test). Tests cover all three policies, including that `discard` deletes the original bytes right after ingestion and keeps only insights and cited quotes.

## 1. Domain (`vault/domain/`, dataclasses and frozen pydantic, stdlib only)
- `ids.py`: `DocumentId`, `ChunkId` NewTypes over UUID. `CHUNK_NS = UUID("6f1c2b8e-0d4a-5b7e-9c31-a2f4e8d0b915")`, `CHUNKER_VERSION = 1`. `chunk_id(doc, ord) = uuid5(CHUNK_NS, f"{doc}:{CHUNKER_VERSION}:{ord}")`, so it is deterministic and stays the same when the job is re-run.
- `enums.py`: `DocKind` (§4.2), `DocumentMime` (`application/pdf`, `application/vnd.openxmlformats-officedocument.wordprocessingml.document`), `DocStatus` = `uploaded, extracting, extracted, analysing, ready, failed, rejected, deleting`, `RetentionPolicy` = `keep|discard|user_choice`, `Retention` = `keep|discard`, `PageBasis` = `physical|page_breaks|none`.
- `document.py` `Document(AggregateRoot)` has the fields `id, owner_id: AccountId, filename, mime, size_bytes, sha256, storage_key, retention: Retention, kind: DocKind | None` (set by the user), `kind_suggested, kind_reason, status, failure_code: str | None, pages, page_basis, chunk_count, extractor: str | None, original_deleted_at, extracted_at, ready_at, deleted_at, created_at, updated_at`.
  The transitions are listed below. Anything else raises `InvalidStateTransition`.
  `upload()`→`uploaded` · `start_extraction` from `uploaded|extracting` (a re-run after a crash is allowed) · `finish_extraction(pages, basis, chunks, extractor)` from `extracting` · `reject(code)` from `extracting` · `start_analysis` from `extracted|analysing` · `finish_analysis(kind_suggested, reason)` from `analysing`, to `ready` · `fail(code)` from `uploaded|extracting|extracted|analysing` · `mark_original_deleted(now)` only when `retention=discard` and the status is `ready|failed|rejected` (idempotent) · `set_kind(k)` in any status except `deleting` · `request_deletion(now)` from every status except `deleting` (it is idempotent). The events `DocumentUploaded, DocumentReady, DocumentRejected, OriginalDiscarded, DocumentDeleted` carry ids only.
  `effective_kind = kind or kind_suggested`. `original_available = retention == keep and original_deleted_at is None`.
- `upload.py` (pure): `sniff_mime(head: bytes, data: bytes) -> DocumentMime | None`. PDF: `%PDF-` appears in the first 1024 bytes. DOCX: starts with `PK\x03\x04`, and the stdlib `zipfile` central directory holds `[Content_Types].xml` and `word/document.xml`. The declared Content-Type and the file extension are ignored. `check_zip(data)`: at most 1000 entries, total uncompressed size at most 200 MB, no entry with a compression ratio above 100, otherwise `FileRejected("zip_bomb")`. `clean_filename(s)` keeps the basename, applies NFC, strips control characters and limits the length to 255 (`""` becomes `document`). `resolve_retention(policy, keep_original: bool | None) -> Retention`: `keep` gives keep, `discard` gives discard, `user_choice` gives keep only when `keep_original is True`.
- `chunking.py` (pure): `chunk_pages(doc_id, pages: Sequence[str], p=ChunkParams(target=3000, max=4000, min=600)) -> list[Chunk]`. Each page is normalised (NFC, `\r\n` to `\n`, control characters except `\n\t` removed, trailing spaces stripped, 3 or more newlines collapsed to 2). Pages are joined with `\n\n` and page spans are recorded. Paragraphs (split on blank lines) are packed greedily up to `target`. A paragraph longer than `max` is split at sentence ends, and failing that at the last whitespace before `max`. A tail shorter than `min` is merged into the previous chunk if the result stays at or below `max`. A chunk may cross pages. `Chunk(id, document_id, ord (0..n-1), label=f"c{ord}", page_from, page_to, char_start, char_end, text)`.
- `insights.py`. The LLM output types match `prompts/InsightExtractor.md`: `DocumentInsights {kind: DocKind, kind_reason, title, aims|findings|open_questions: list[Item{summary, chunk_ids}], methods: list[Method{name, detail, chunk_ids}], datasets: list[Dataset{name, status: available|planned|partial, chunk_ids}], reviewer_objections: list[Objection{quote, summary, chunk_ids}], keywords: list[str] (max 12)}`. Here `chunk_ids` are the labels (`c12`). Lists have at most 8 items (`max_length`).
  The stored form is `InsightItem {section, data: dict, chunk_labels, chunk_ids: tuple[ChunkId, ...], status: verified|unverified, reason: UnverifiedReason | None, stage: full|map|reduce}`. `StoredInsights {document_id, items, keywords, title, kind_suggested, kind_reason, prompt_version ("InsightExtractor@1.0.0"), model, mode: full|map_reduce, slices, cost_usd}`. `visible()` returns verified items only.
- `verification.py` (pure): `verify(raw, chunks_by_label, stage) -> list[InsightItem]`. An item is unverified when it has no `chunk_ids` (`no_chunk_ref`), when any label is unknown (`unknown_chunk`, strict), or when its summary contains an email address (`personal_data`). An objection is also unverified when `norm(quote)` has fewer than 15 characters (`quote_too_short`) or is not a substring of `norm(" ".join(cited chunk texts in ord order))` (`quote_not_found`). `norm` applies NFKC, maps curly quotes to straight quotes, maps U+2010-2015 and U+2212 to `-`, removes soft hyphens, rewrites `-\s+` as `-`, and collapses whitespace to single spaces. Case is kept.
- `retention.py` (pure): `retained_content(chunks, items) -> dict[ChunkId, Retained(quotes: tuple[Quote{text, truncated}], summary)]`. A quote comes from a **verified** objection and is attached to its first cited chunk. It is `norm(quote)` cut to at most 300 characters at a word boundary, with `truncated=True` when cut. The summary is the `" · "`-joined summaries of the verified items that cite the chunk, at most 300 characters. Chunks that nobody cites keep only their ids, offsets and page numbers.
- `slicing.py` (pure): `plan_slices(chunks, full_max=120_000, slice_chars=60_000) -> list[list[Chunk]]`. A document whose total text is at most `full_max` is sent as one slice in `full` mode. Longer documents are split into greedy consecutive slices, and a chunk is never split.

## 2. Ports
- `scout/ports/storage.py` (shared, because M6 account deletion needs it). `FileStoragePort`: `put(key, data, content_type)` (overwrites), `get(key) -> bytes` (raises `StorageObjectMissing(InfrastructureError, http 404)`), `delete(key)` (a missing key is fine), `delete_prefix(prefix) -> int`, `exists(key) -> bool`. Keys match `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9/_-]*$`, and `..` or a leading `/` raises `ValidationFailed`. The vault key is `vault/{owner_id}/{document_id}` (§10) and never contains the filename.

It raises `ExtractionRejected(DomainError, 422)` with a `code` in `encrypted|no_text_layer|too_many_pages|text_too_long|corrupt_file|zip_bomb|extraction_timeout|too_complex`. Permanent errors never retry.
- `vault/ports/insights.py`. `InsightExtractorPort.extract(req: InsightRequest, ctx: CallContext) -> InsightOutcome`. `InsightRequest {mode: full|map|reduce, filename, kind_hint, chunks: tuple[ChunkInput{chunk_id(label), page, text}], partials: tuple[DocumentInsights]}`. `InsightOutcome {insights, prompt_version, model, cost_usd}`. It raises `LLMUnavailable` / `LLMOutputInvalid`, which are transient and retried by the job.
- `vault/ports/repos.py`.

  - `ChunkRepo`: `replace_all(doc_id, chunks)`, `list(doc_id)`, `redact(doc_id, retained, now) -> int`, `delete_all(doc_id) -> int`.
  - `InsightsRepo`: `get(doc_id)`, `upsert(s)`, `delete(doc_id)`.
  - `VaultUnitOfWork`: `documents, chunks, insights, audit`.
- Fakes in `tests/fakes/`: `storage.py` `InMemoryFileStorage` (with `fail_next(op, n)`), `vault.py` (in-memory repos and UoW), `extraction.py` `FakeTextExtractor` (UTF-8 bytes, `\f` separates pages, `script_reject(code)`). The insight port uses the real `AgentsInsightExtractor` over `FakeLLM` with `script()`.
- Contract tests, shared between fake and real adapters. `tests/contract/test_file_storage.py` runs over memory, local and supabase (`needs_supabase`): round trip, overwrite, missing get, idempotent delete, prefix delete removes only the prefix, key validation. `tests/contract/test_text_extraction.py` runs over `PdfDocxExtractor` (real files generated at test time with `reportlab`/`python-docx`, dev dependencies) and the fake for the common subset: page count, quote found on page 9, the rejections.

## 3. Adapters
- `vault/adapters/extraction.py` `PdfDocxExtractor` (DECISIONS 46). It uses **pypdf** for PDF (`page.extract_text()` per physical page; empty-password decryption is tried, otherwise `encrypted`) and **python-docx** for DOCX (pages split at `w:br type=page` and `w:lastRenderedPageBreak`, which gives `page_breaks`, or `none` with everything on page 1; table cells are appended row by row with ` | `). Extraction runs in a **child process** (`python -m scout.vault.adapters.extract_child`, bytes on stdin, JSON on stdout) with `RLIMIT_AS = VAULT_EXTRACT_MEMORY_MB` and `RLIMIT_CPU`. On a wall-clock timeout of `VAULT_EXTRACT_TIMEOUT_S` the child is killed and the result is `extraction_timeout`. Limits: `pages > VAULT_MAX_PAGES` gives `too_many_pages`. Total characters above `VAULT_MAX_TEXT_CHARS = 720_000` (12 slices) give `text_too_long`. Fewer than 200 characters on a document with at least 1 page gives `no_text_layer`, because there is no OCR in the MVP. **Fallback** (inside the child process): if pypdf raises, or returns fewer than 200 characters, the PDF is parsed again with **pdfplumber** before it is rejected. `extractor` records which parser was used (`pypdf@6`, `pdfplumber@0.11`, `python-docx@1`). The library evaluation is in §14.
- `scout/adapters/storage/local.py` `LocalFileStorage(root)`: atomic tmp write then `os.replace`, mode 0600. `supabase.py` `SupabaseStorage(httpx, url, service_key, bucket="vault", usage)`: REST `object/{bucket}/{key}` with `x-upsert`, `object/authenticated/...`, `DELETE object/{bucket} {prefixes}`, `object/list`. It has a 30 s timeout, retries 5xx/429 3 times, and writes one `external_call` per request (`service=storage`, endpoint = op, no key).
- `vault/adapters/insight_extractor.py` `AgentsInsightExtractor(factory)` sends `spec("InsightExtractor", DocumentInsights)` with the request as JSON input.
- `vault/adapters/sql.py` `SqlVaultUnitOfWork`, with the SQLModel tables in `adapters/persistence/models.py`.

## 4. Agent factory (`scout/adapters/llm/factory.py`, plan B5)
`AgentFactory(llm: LLMPort, prompts_dir: Path, placeholders: Mapping[str, str], tracer)`:
- `spec(name, output_type, **overrides) -> AgentSpec` is cached per name. It uses the existing `load_agent_spec` and also fills `{{key}}` from `placeholders` (`field_name`, `today`). An unknown or unfilled placeholder raises `ValidationFailed`. Bootstrap calls `spec()` for every wired agent, so a broken prompt fails at startup. The version is stored as `f"{name}@{version}"`.
- The tier comes from the front-matter (`fast|reasoning`). The adapters already map the tier to a route (`LLM_MODEL_FAST/REASONING` → llm-router route). The factory never picks models.
- `run(spec, input, ctx: CallContext)` wraps `llm.run` in the span `agent.run`. Its attributes are name, prompt version, tier, route, model, attempts, tokens, `cost_usd` and `input_chars`, and it ends with `log.info("agent_done", ...)` with the same fields. **Input and output text are never put in spans, logs, exceptions or `job.last_error`.**
- Error mapping. `LLMOutputInvalid` and pydantic `ValidationError` are re-raised as `LLMOutputInvalid(f"{name}: output invalid")`. Any other `InfrastructureError` (including `FakeFixtureMissing`) and any `httpx`/provider error become `LLMUnavailable(f"{name}: unavailable")`. Both are raised **`from None`**, because a chained `ValidationError` embeds the vault text. Programming errors propagate. Metering stays in the adapters (one `llm_call` per attempt, `CallContext.feature = "vault.insights"`, `account_id`, `session_id`).
- `OpenAIAgentsDirectAdapter` keeps `trace_include_sensitive_data=False` and is built with `set_tracing_disabled(True)`, so no prompts are exported to the OpenAI trace store.

## 5. Use cases (`vault/application/`) and jobs
- **`UploadDocument.upload(owner_id, filename, data, keep_original: bool | None, kind_hint, session_id) -> (Document, created: bool)`**:
  (1) Check the size (`0 < len <= VAULT_MAX_BYTES`, otherwise `FileTooLarge` 413), sniff the type (otherwise `UnsupportedFileType` 415), run `check_zip` for DOCX, compute the sha256.
  (2) In a read UoW: if `find_active_by_sha` finds a document, return it with `created=False`. If `count_active >= VAULT_MAX_DOCUMENTS` raise `Conflict("vault_limit_reached")`.
  (3) Compute the retention, the id and the key, then `storage.put` outside any UoW.
  (4) In a UoW: `add` the document and the audit row `document.uploaded {document_id, mime, size_bytes, retention}`, then commit. On `DuplicateDocument`, delete the new key and return the existing document. On any other error, delete the key on a best-effort basis; if that delete fails, log the error `vault.orphan_object {key}`; then re-raise.
  (5) `enqueue(JobSpec("vault.ingest", {account_id, session_id, feature: "vault.ingest", document_id}, dedupe_key=str(id), max_attempts=4))`. If the enqueue fails it is logged and the request still returns 201; the sweeper recovers the document.
- **`IngestDocument.handle(payload, *, last_attempt) -> dict`** (the `vault.ingest` handler). The current state decides the next step, so the job is at-least-once and resumable:
  - The document is missing or `deleting`: `{"skipped": "gone"}`.
  - `uploaded|extracting` → **extract**. UoW `start_extraction` and commit. `storage.get` and `extract_text` run outside any UoW, then `chunk_pages`. A UoW then locks the row and checks that the status is still `extracting`, runs `chunks.replace_all` and `finish_extraction`, and commits. `ExtractionRejected` leads to `reject(code)` and audit `document.rejected`, then **purge**. `StorageObjectMissing` on a document that is still active is permanent: `fail("original_missing")`.
  - `extracted|analysing` → **analyse**. UoW `start_analysis` and commit. `ExtractInsights` runs (below). A UoW then locks the row and checks the status is still `analysing`. It upserts the insights, calls `finish_analysis`, and **if `retention=discard` calls `chunks.redact(retained_content(...))` in the same transaction**, then commits. Then **purge**.
  - **purge** (when `retention=discard`, the status is `ready|failed|rejected` and `original_deleted_at is None`): `storage.delete(key)`, then a UoW runs `mark_original_deleted` and audit `document.original_discarded {document_id, chunks_redacted}`.
  - Otherwise `{"skipped": "done"}`.
  - Errors: `InfrastructureError` re-raises, and the worker backs off. On `last_attempt` it first runs `fail("insights_unavailable" | "extraction_failed" | "storage_unavailable")` in its own UoW, plus chunk redaction and purge for discard, then re-raises.
- **`ExtractInsights.run(doc, chunks, ctx) -> StoredInsights`** (application):
  - The slices come from `plan_slices`.
  - With one slice, it makes a single call in `full` mode.
  - With several, it runs one `map` call per slice, at most 3 at a time (`asyncio.Semaphore`). Each partial is checked with `verify(stage="map")`. The verified items go to a single `reduce` call, which receives `partials` and no chunks. The unverified map items are kept with `stage=map`.
  - The final output is verified against **all** chunks.
  - Labels are global (`c{ord}`), so ids survive the reduce step.
  - The cost is summed over the calls. A 30-page proposal (about 90k characters) takes one fast call.
- **`SetDocumentKind(owner, id, kind)`**: get it, or raise `NotFound`, then `set_kind` and commit. There is no audit row.
- **`DeleteDocument(owner, id)`**: one UoW with `get_for_update` and an owner check (another owner's document raises `NotFound`). It runs `request_deletion`, `chunks.delete_all`, `insights.delete` and the audit row `document.deleted {document_id, retention, original_present}`, then commits, so **content is gone at commit**. It then enqueues `vault.delete` (`dedupe_key=id`) and returns 204 even if the enqueue fails. The **`vault.delete`** handler: the document is missing, so done, or its status is not `deleting`, so skipped. Otherwise it runs `storage.delete(key)`, then a UoW with `hard_delete` and commits. On failure it retries with back-off, and every failure logs the error `vault.storage_delete_failed {document_id, attempt}`. The `deleting` row itself acts as the outbox entry: it is not removed until the storage object is gone.
- **`SweepVault`** (job `vault.sweep`) reschedules itself every 10 min, with `dedupe_key` set to the slot time, like `maintenance.ensure_partitions`. It is scheduled when the worker starts. It enqueues `vault.ingest` (deduped) for documents that are `uploaded` and older than 10 min, and for documents with `retention=discard`, a terminal status and `original_deleted_at is None` older than 10 min. It enqueues `vault.delete` for `deleting` rows older than 10 min. It marks `extracting|analysing` rows older than 1 h as `fail("ingest_stalled")` and purges them if discard. It handles at most 100 rows per kind and logs ids and counts.

## 6. Retention behaviour (D6, ADR-009)
| Policy | The `keep_original` form field | Bytes | `document_chunk.text` after the terminal status | GET |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `keep` | ignored | kept until DELETE | kept | `retention=keep, original_available=true` |
| `discard` | ignored | deleted right after `ready/failed/rejected` (purge step); audit `document.original_discarded` | NULL; `quotes` (verified, at most 300 chars) + `summary` | `retention=discard, original_available=false` |
| `user_choice` (default) | `true` gives keep; absent or `false` gives discard ("keep original" unticked) | as chosen | as chosen | as chosen |

`retention` is fixed at upload, and in M2 PATCH cannot change it (keep→discard is in BACKLOG). The insight items keep their chunk ids and pages for both values. For M3: evidence for a discarded document can only quote the stored `quotes` (§16).

## 7. Persistence: Alembic `0004_vault` (plain PG16; RLS in the Supabase branch through `EXISTS` on `document.owner_id`)
- `document`: `id uuid pk, owner_id uuid not null fk account on delete cascade, filename text, mime text check, size_bytes bigint check > 0, sha256 char(64), storage_key text, retention text check (keep, discard), kind text null check, kind_suggested text null check, kind_reason text, status text check (8 values), failure_code text, pages int, page_basis text check, chunk_count int, extractor text, original_deleted_at, extracted_at, ready_at, deleted_at, created_at, updated_at`. Unique index `(owner_id, sha256) WHERE status NOT IN ('failed','rejected','deleting')`, index `(owner_id, created_at DESC) WHERE status <> 'deleting'`, index `(status, updated_at)`.
- `document_chunk`: `id uuid pk (uuid5), document_id fk on delete cascade, ord int, label text, page_from int, page_to int check >= page_from, char_start int, char_end int, text text null, quotes jsonb not null default '[]', summary text, redacted_at timestamptz, created_at, updated_at`. Unique `(document_id, ord)`, check `text IS NOT NULL OR redacted_at IS NOT NULL`.
- `document_insights`: `document_id uuid pk fk on delete cascade` (1:1, so there is no separate id; this deviates from §7), `payload jsonb` (all items with status, reason and stage), `verified_count int, unverified_count int, prompt_version text, model text, mode text, slices int, cost_usd numeric(12,8), created_at, updated_at`.

## 10. Invariants (tests assert)
1. Only the transitions in §1. `deleting` is final. `ready|failed|rejected` never go back to an in-progress status.
2. `retention=discard` and a status in `ready|failed|rejected` imply that every chunk of the document has `text IS NULL`, and that each stored quote is at most 300 characters and comes from a verified objection. After the purge step `storage.exists(key)` is false and exactly one `document.original_discarded` audit row exists.
3. User-facing payloads (`GET /documents/{id}`) never contain an unverified item. A verified item's `chunk_ids` all belong to the document. A verified objection's `norm(quote)` is a substring of its cited chunks.
4. Chunking: ords are contiguous; `len(text) <= max`; `page_from <= page_to` and both are monotonic; the concatenated non-whitespace text equals the input's non-whitespace text; the same input gives the same ids.
5. At most one active document per `(owner_id, sha256)`. Re-POSTing the same bytes returns the same id.
6. Owner isolation: GET, PATCH and DELETE on another owner's document answer 404 and change nothing.
7. After `DELETE`, no chunk or insight row of the document exists at commit. After `vault.delete`, neither the document row nor the storage object exists.
8. Running `vault.ingest` twice, or crashing after any step and re-running, gives the same chunks and insights and at most one purge audit row.
9. No vault text appears in logs, spans, `job.last_error` or exceptions. A canary string in a document is searched for in all of them.

## 13. Risks and choices
- **Retention timing.** The `discard` policy keeps the full text in Postgres while ingestion runs, for about 2 min, because the extractor needs it. Bytes and text are purged at the terminal status, including `failed`, so a failed discard document must be uploaded again (a retry endpoint is in BACKLOG).
- **Chunks across slices.** A quote that spans two chunks verifies against their concatenation. A quote split across map slices may fail verification and stays unverified. This is accepted.
- **Undecided, needs the product owner.** Whether a `failed` document in a `keep` vault should get a "retry" button. Whether the 50-document cap is right for the pilot.
- **Text extraction choice.** pypdf by default, with pdfplumber as the fallback (DECISIONS 46, §14). Scanned PDFs are `rejected: no_text_layer` until docling or OCR is added (BACKLOG). DOCX page numbers exist only when the file carries page breaks, otherwise `page_basis=none`, and the UI must then say "section" rather than "page".

**Choice (DECISIONS 46).**
- The default is `PdfDocxExtractor` = **pypdf** for PDF and **python-docx** for DOCX. It is about 25 times faster than the alternatives, small, pure Python for PDF, and gives exact page provenance for `page_from`/`page_to`.
- The fallback is **pdfplumber**, only inside the child process: when pypdf raises a parse error, or returns fewer than 200 characters on a PDF whose pages contain font resources, before the `corrupt_file` or `no_text_layer` rejection.
- markitdown is not adopted. It adds no OCR, loses DOCX pages, is 25 times slower on PDF than pypdf and is 10 times heavier.
- docling is deferred (BACKLOG). It is the only path to OCR and table structure, but it needs a CPU torch source, pre-baked model weights and its own worker image.
- `TextExtractionPort` stays as specified in §2. Any of these could be adapted to it: Markdown output is plain text per page, and docling's `prov.page_no` would fill `pages`.
